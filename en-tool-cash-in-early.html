What you get if you cash in early

If you suddenly need the money, see what you get and what you lose before you cash in. At the official rates, plainly.

What you get at each point

    You can cash in before maturity, but the government then pays a lower rate and deducts any extra profit already paid from your principal. Try your own amount in the calculator above; the official rates for each are below.

    Three things to remember

    • Wage Earner Bond: no profit if cashed within 6 months.
    • Dollar bonds: no profit if cashed within 1 year.
    • Sanchayapatra: the 7.5 lakh limit counts all your certificates together, and the official tables do not say what you get within the first year.

    How it is worked out: simple profit at the official rate, for the complete periods you held it (6 months at a time for the Wage Earner Bond, a year at a time for the others). It is an estimate; your bank or the Savings Bureau sets the final amount.

    If you might need the money, split it across several certificates instead of one. Then you only cash in one when needed, and the rest keep earning the full rate.

    Questions people ask

    How much do I get if I cash in Sanchayapatra early?

    Profit at the official rate for the years you held it, for example 10.21% after 2 years on the 5-year certificate up to 7.5 lakh. Extra profit already paid is deducted from the principal, and 10% tax comes off the profit.

    What happens if I cash in the Wage Earner Bond early?

    No profit within 6 months. After that a lower rate by holding time, for example 11.20% from 2 to 5 years up to 15 lakh, against 12% at maturity.

    What if I cash in a dollar bond within a year?

    Both the Premium and the Investment Bond pay no profit within the first year; you get only your principal back.
