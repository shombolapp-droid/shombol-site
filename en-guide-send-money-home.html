The best ways to send money home to Bangladesh

Every taka is hard-earned. Send it legally and the government adds a bonus, your family receives it safely, and you keep proof of your income.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 4 min

There are three legal ways to send money home: a bank, a licensed exchange house, or a licensed money transfer app. All three earn the government's incentive. The 2026-27 budget keeps that incentive at 2.5%.

Which way from which country

  • Middle East (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman): licensed exchange houses or the overseas exchange partners of Bangladeshi banks. Many people now use their apps.
  • Malaysia and Singapore: licensed remittance companies and their apps, or a bank transfer.
  • Europe, the UK and the US: a bank transfer or a licensed money transfer app. Compare both the fee and the exchange rate before you send.

How the 2.5% incentive works

When money arrives through a legal channel, the bank in Bangladesh adds 2.5% on top for the receiver. Send 1 lakh taka and your family receives 1 lakh 2,500 taka. For large amounts the bank may ask for income documents.

How to pay less

  • Sending larger amounts less often usually means lower total fees.
  • Look at the exchange rate, not just the fee. A low fee with a poor rate costs more.
  • Send straight to a bank account, so you keep both the incentive and the proof.

Hundi may look like a slightly better rate, but there is no government incentive, no proof of your income, and no legal protection if the money is lost. It also hurts the country's economy.

To decide what to do with the money you send, read the Wage Earner Bond and NFCD account guides.

Questions people ask

What is the government remittance incentive now?

2.5%. The 2026-27 budget keeps it.

Which app is safe to send money with?

One that is licensed in your country and partnered with a bank in Bangladesh. Never send through a stranger or an agent you met on social media.

What is wrong with hundi?

You lose the incentive, you have no proof of income, and there is no legal protection if the money is lost.
