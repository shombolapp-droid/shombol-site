How to open an NFCD (FC) account from abroad

A safe home for your money in Bangladesh. It stays in foreign currency, and it is the easiest place to buy bonds from.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 4 min

If you send money home into an ordinary savings account, it turns into taka and is hard to take back abroad later. An NFCD (Non-resident Foreign Currency Deposit) account keeps your money in foreign currency. Almost every safe investment for expatriates starts here.

Why you need this account

  • Your money stays in dollars: if the taka weakens, your savings keep their value.
  • Buying bonds is easy: dollar bonds and the Wage Earner Bond can be paid for straight from this account.
  • You can take it back: money that came from abroad can go back abroad under the rules.
  • Proof of income: every legal transfer leaves a record at the bank, which you will need later.

Documents you need

  • A copy of your valid passport
  • Visa, work permit or proof that you live abroad
  • A recent photo
  • Your nominee's photo, NID or passport copy and details
  • Proof of income, such as a job ID or payslip

How to open it, step by step

  1. Choose a bankAny Authorised Dealer (AD) branch in Bangladesh opens NFCD accounts. A bank with a branch or exchange house in your country makes life easier later.
  2. ApplySome banks take applications through their overseas branches, exchange houses or online. Ask your bank which route it accepts.
  3. Send your first depositSend foreign currency from abroad through a bank or a licensed exchange house and deposit it into the account.
  4. Keep the receiptsKeep every transfer receipt and bank statement. You will need them to buy bonds and to take money back abroad.

Sending money through legal channels earns the government's 2.5% incentive and builds proof of your income. With hundi you lose both, and take on risk.

What to do once it is open

As a term deposit you can choose from 3 months to 5 years, and each bank now sets its own interest rate.

Keep your emergency money aside, then consider the dollar bonds or the Wage Earner Bond for the rest. Both are government guaranteed and tax-free.

Questions people ask

Does an NFCD account pay interest?

Yes, term NFCD deposits earn interest. Since 2022 there is no rate ceiling and each bank sets its own, so ask your bank before you open one.

Can I open it without coming home?

Some banks accept applications online or through their overseas branches and exchange houses. The process differs by bank, so ask the bank directly.

Can my family withdraw from this account?

The account is in your name. Your nominee receives the money if anything happens to you. For family expenses, sending money to a separate taka account is simpler.
