How to buy Sanchayapatra in your family's name

A sure monthly income for your parents or spouse at home. How to buy it, and the tax rule that changed in 2026.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 5 min

Regular Sanchayapatra needs a Bangladeshi NID and a local bank account. That is why many people abroad buy it in a parent's or spouse's name, so the monthly profit goes straight to them. You send the money; the certificate is in their name.

What changed in tax in 2026

From 1 July 2026, 10% advance tax is deducted at source from the profit of every kind of Sanchayapatra. Before, holdings up to 5 lakh taka had 5% deducted and that was the final tax. Now it is an advance: someone whose income is below the taxable limit can get the extra back by getting a TIN and filing a tax return.

Example: 1 lakh taka in the Family Sanchayapatra earns about ৳994 a month. After 10% tax, about ৳895 reaches the account.

Who can buy the Family Sanchayapatra

  • Women aged 18 or over
  • Women and men with a physical disability
  • Women and men aged 65 or over

How to buy, step by step

  1. Their documentsA National ID (NID), a bank account in their own name, a photo and nominee details.
  2. Above 10 lakh: proof of returnPurchases above 10 lakh taka need proof of a filed tax return (PSR). Under the new rule, a TIN and return are also how tax is refunded.
  3. Send the moneySend it legally to their bank account, so the source of the money is clear.
  4. Apply onlineApply as e-Sanchayapatra at a bank, post office or National Savings Bureau. Profit goes straight to the bank account.

Keep each person's holding within 7.5 lakh taka instead of buying everything in one name, and you get the higher rate.

The certificate is in their name, so legally it is their property. Talk openly with your family and keep a written record.

Questions people ask

Can expatriates buy Sanchayapatra themselves?

Regular Sanchayapatra needs a Bangladeshi NID and a local bank account. The Wage Earner Bond and dollar bonds, made for expatriates, can be bought with your passport, and they are tax-free.

What is the Sanchayapatra profit rate in 2026?

For July to December 2026: Family 11.93%, 5-year 11.83%, 3-monthly 11.82%, Pensioner 11.98% (up to 7.5 lakh taka).

How much tax is now taken from Sanchayapatra profit?

From 1 July 2026, 10% advance tax. If the income is not taxable, the extra can be refunded by getting a TIN and filing a return.
