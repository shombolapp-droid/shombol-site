How to buy the Wage Earner Development Bond

A government bond made only for Bangladeshis abroad. The highest tax-free return in taka, and a steady income for the family every 6 months.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 4 min

If you work abroad and want a steady income for your family at home, the Wage Earner Development Bond (WEDB) is probably the best government option for you. Profit and principal are both tax-free, which Sanchayapatra is not.

Who can buy it

  • Any Bangladeshi working abroad
  • Bangladeshi sailors, pilots and cabin crew working for foreign shipping lines or airlines
  • Government staff working abroad on lien, and Bangladesh mission staff
  • The money must be foreign income sent through legal channels. It can also be bought in the name of a family member at home

How to buy, step by step

  1. Where to buyAn overseas branch or exchange house of a Bangladeshi bank, or an Authorised Dealer (AD) bank branch at home.
  2. Prepare the documentsA passport copy, visa or work permit, a photo, and your nominee's photo and details.
  3. PayFrom remittance or your NFCD account. The bond is bought in taka.
  4. Add a nomineeAlways add one, so your family can claim the money easily if anything happens to you.
  5. Keep the certificateCollect the certificate or proof of online registration and keep it safe.

Stay within 15 lakh taka and all of it earns 12%. Profit arrives in taka every 6 months, which helps with regular family expenses. At maturity you can reinvest twice, for up to 15 years in total.

Cash in within 6 months and you get no profit.

To see your own numbers, choose the Wage Earner Bond in the calculator and enter an amount.

Questions people ask

Is there a maximum investment in the Wage Earner Bond?

No, the government removed the cap. Larger amounts earn less: 12% up to 15 lakh, 11% for 15 to 30 lakh, 10% for 30 to 50 lakh, 9% above 50 lakh.

Is tax deducted from Wage Earner Bond profit?

No. Principal and profit are both fully tax-free.

How is profit paid?

Every 6 months, in taka, into the bank account you name.
