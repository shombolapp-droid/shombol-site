How to buy Bangladesh bonds from Saudi Arabia

How to keep the money you earn in Saudi Arabia safe at home, tax-free and government guaranteed, step by step.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 4 min

Living in Saudi Arabia and want your money to grow safely back home? The government has three bonds made for people abroad, all tax-free and guaranteed by the Government of Bangladesh. In FY 2025-26, $5.85 billion came home from Saudi Arabia, more than from any other country.

The bonds you can buy

BondRate (at maturity)TermProfit
Wage Earner Development Bond12%5 yearsIn taka, every 6 months
US Dollar Premium Bond7.5%3 yearsIn taka, every 6 months
US Dollar Investment Bond6.5%3 yearsIn dollars, every 6 months

Lowest-tier rates: the Wage Earner Bond up to 15 lakh taka, dollar bonds up to $100,000. Larger amounts earn a little less.

How to buy from Saudi Arabia

  1. Open an NFCD account at homeDollar bonds need a non-resident FC (NFCD) account in Bangladesh in your name. See how to open one.
  2. Send money legallyFrom saudi arabia through a bank, a Bangladeshi bank's exchange house or a licensed money transfer company. The list is below.
  3. Gather the documentsA passport copy, a copy of your iqama (Resident Identity), payslips or remittance receipts, a photo, and your nominee's photo and NID.
  4. Buy the bondThrough an overseas branch or exchange house of a Bangladeshi bank, or any Authorised Dealer (AD) bank branch at home. The Wage Earner Bond can also be bought in a family member's name at home.
  5. Keep the certificateCollect the certificate or proof of online registration, and always add a nominee.

Ways to send money from Saudi Arabia

  • Al Rajhi Bank (Tahweel Al Rajhi): supervised by the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA); a partner on Sonali Bank's July 2026 list; pays straight into Bangladeshi bank accounts.
  • Bank Albilad: a partner on Sonali Bank's July 2026 list; pays straight into Bangladeshi bank accounts.
  • Arab National Bank: a partner on Sonali Bank's July 2026 list; pays straight into Bangladeshi bank accounts.
  • Alinma Bank: a partner on Sonali Bank's July 2026 list; pays straight into Bangladeshi bank accounts.

Legal channels earn the government's 2.5% incentive. We could not confirm any Bangladeshi-bank-owned exchange house operating in Saudi Arabia in 2026, so the Saudi banks above are the simplest route.

Bangladesh missions

The Bangladesh Embassy in Riyadh (riyadh.mofa.gov.bd) and the Consulate General in Jeddah (jeddah.mofa.gov.bd). Missions do not sell bonds; you buy them through a bank.

Never hand money to an individual or a social media "agent". Government bonds are sold only by banks and their exchange houses. How to spot a scam.

Questions people ask

Can I buy the Wage Earner Bond directly from Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Through an overseas branch or exchange house of a Bangladeshi bank, or by sending the money home and buying at an AD bank branch. It can also be bought in a family member's name.

Which documents do I need?

A passport copy, a copy of your iqama (Resident Identity), proof of income or remittance receipts, a photo, and your nominee's photo and NID.

What is the safe way to send money from Saudi Arabia?

A bank, a Bangladeshi bank's exchange house or a licensed money transfer company. Hundi gives you no incentive, no proof and no protection.
