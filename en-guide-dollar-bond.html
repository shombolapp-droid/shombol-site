How to buy Bangladesh dollar bonds from abroad

Tax-free profit on money kept in dollars, guaranteed by the government. The difference between the two bonds and how to buy them.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 5 min

If you want to keep your money in dollars, the government offers two bonds. Both run for 3 years, are tax-free and are guaranteed by the Government of Bangladesh. The one difference: the currency your profit is paid in.

Which one suits you

  • US Dollar Premium Bond: the higher rate, but profit is paid in taka. Good if the profit will cover family expenses at home.
  • US Dollar Investment Bond: a slightly lower rate, but principal and profit are both in dollars. Good if you want to take all of it abroad later.

Who can buy them

  • Bangladeshi citizens living abroad
  • Foreign citizens of Bangladeshi origin living abroad (dual nationals)
  • Condition: you need a non-resident FC (NFCD) account in Bangladesh in your name

How to buy, step by step

  1. Choose where to buyAn overseas branch of a Bangladeshi bank, a licensed exchange house, or any Authorised Dealer (AD) bank branch in Bangladesh.
  2. The money must be foreign incomeIt must come from remittance sent from abroad or from your NFCD account.
  3. Hand in the documentsA passport copy, visa or work permit, remittance receipts or proof of income, a photo, and your nominee's photo and NID.
  4. Choose the amountThe Premium Bond starts at $500. Up to $100,000 gets the highest rate.
  5. Collect the certificateAfter buying, always collect the certificate or proof of online registration.

Profit is paid every 6 months. Premium Bond profit is in taka and cannot be taken abroad. Investment Bond profit is in dollars.

Cash in before 1 year and you get no profit at all. Only use money you will not need for at least 3 years.

Questions people ask

Premium Bond or Investment Bond: which is better?

For the higher return, the Premium Bond (7.5%), with profit paid in taka. To take everything abroad in dollars, profit included, the Investment Bond (6.5%).

Is tax deducted from dollar bond profit?

No. Principal and profit of both dollar bonds are tax-free.

What if I need the money before maturity?

You can cash in, but there is no profit within the first year, and a lower rate after that.
