Sanchayapatra profit rates 2026: July to December

The rates are unchanged. But from 1 July, 10% advance tax is taken from the profit, so a little less reaches your account.

Facts checked: 20 November 2026Reading time: 3 min

Under the Finance Ministry's notice of 2 July 2026, Sanchayapatra profit rates stay the same for July to December 2026. A rate cut was announced for January 2026, but on 4 January 2026 the government withdrew it and kept the earlier rates.

The new tax rule

Under the Finance Act 2026, from 1 July 2026 10% advance tax is deducted at source from the profit of every kind of Sanchayapatra. Before, holdings up to 5 lakh taka had 5% deducted, and that was the final tax. Now it is an advance: someone whose income is not taxable can get the extra back by getting a TIN and filing a tax return.

Questions people ask

Did Sanchayapatra rates go down in 2026?

No. The January 2026 cut was withdrawn, and rates are unchanged for July to December 2026 too.

How much does 1 lakh in the Family Sanchayapatra pay per month?

৳994 before tax, about ৳895 after the 10% advance tax.

When will new Sanchayapatra rates be announced?

The government reviews them every six months. The next review is in January 2027.

Will the rate on certificates I already own change?

No. The purchase rate holds until maturity. The new tax rule does apply to all profit paid from 1 July 2026.
